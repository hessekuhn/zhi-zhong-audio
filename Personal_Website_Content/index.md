<div class="bio-copy">

Zhi Zhong is a Senior Researcher at Sony's Creative AI Lab.

His research focuses on AI systems that understand, restore, edit, and generate audio and other modalities.
His work spans representation learning and deep generative modeling.

He received an M.S. from the Institute of Science Tokyo (former Tokyo Tech) and a B.Eng. from Huazhong University of Science and Technology. 

A complete publication list is available on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=iRVT3A8AAAAJ). He is also on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/zhongzhiaudio).

</div>

## Research Interests
<div class="home-interest-block">

<article class="home-interest-card home-interest-card--approach">
<h3>Deep Generative Modeling</h3>
<p>Autoregressive and Masked (Diffusion) Language Modeling beyond text. Flow-matching and Diffusion models. GAN. VAE &amp; VQVAE.</p>
</article>

<article class="home-interest-card home-interest-card--approach">
<h3>Representation Learning</h3>
<p>Self-supervised learning &amp; semantic encoder, cross-modal semantic encoder, and neural tokenizer for speech, audio, and music.</p>
</article>

<article class="home-interest-card home-interest-card--approach">
<h3>Multimodal LLMs</h3>
<p>Formulating multi-modal understanding, retrieval, and generative tasks as sequential modeling tasks that can be unified with Language Models.</p>
</article>

<article class="home-interest-card home-interest-card--task">
<h3>Generation</h3>
<p>Text-to-sound. Video-to-sound. Unified sound-to-image &amp; image-to-sound. MIDI-to-audio rendering. Real-time and streaming interactive audio creation.</p>
</article>

<article class="home-interest-card home-interest-card--task">
<h3>Understanding</h3>
<p>Audio &amp; music tagging. Multi-modal retrieval. Timestamped music analysis (e.g., music transcription).</p>
</article>

<article class="home-interest-card home-interest-card--task">
<h3>Restoration &amp; Editing</h3>
<p>Generative restoration of speech, audio, and music. Generative music source separation. Unified inpainting and outpainting for audio and image. Unified music transcription and rendering.</p>
</article>

</div>
